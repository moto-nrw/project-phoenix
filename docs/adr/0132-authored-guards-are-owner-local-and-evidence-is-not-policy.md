# Authored guards are owner-local and evidence is not policy

Decision of 2026-10-28, authorized by the maintainer after the parallel-PR
workflow investigation. Parallel implementation and Quorum remain unchanged.

Authored error identities, architecture permissions, reviewed command effects,
method exceptions and route contracts use stable domain/owner/resource files.
Loaders discover all fragments and assemble the complete input before global
validation. Pure generated derivatives are reproducible, not manually merged.
Observed measurements never authorize a budget or a behavioral expectation.

The architecture-check owner exposes a storage-only contract at
`architecture/artifacts`. Engineering test-support may consume this contract
instead of importing the architecture application. It grants no runtime
domain permission, adds no owner and changes no table or tenant boundary.
Historical monolithic policy documents remain readable for immutable-base
comparison. Fragmented bases are discovered and read from the resolved Git
commit, never from candidate files or candidate symlinks.

The pass-through scanner's scope changes only because policy metadata storage
and its complete readers become part of the hashed source surface. Pure
fragment writers do not select or classify scanned methods and stay outside
that fingerprint; read composition and immutable Git selection remain inside. The exported
method predicate, scan roots, eligibility, debt forms, exact evidence and
removal checks remain unchanged. This newly numbered decision authorizes that
storage transition, not additional debt. Frozen reviewed records must survive
the cutover exactly; newly observed sites stay unclassified until reviewed.

Blind union merges and automatic snapshot/budget updates were rejected because
they can accept contradictory identities or turn a regression into its own
expected result. Repeated full base builds per PR were rejected because they
duplicate CI work; reusable measurements must be source/configuration-bound.
